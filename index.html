Privacy Policy — Taash: Local Bluetooth Cards

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 · Developer Contact: amanjoshi668@gmail.com

Taash (com.taash.taash_app) is a free, offline-first multiplayer card tabletop app for playing classic 52-card Indian games locally over Bluetooth. All core features work in Offline Guest Mode without creating an account.

1. Information We Collect & Permissions

  • Bluetooth Nearby Permissions: Used strictly to discover nearby friends' hosted card tables and exchange game turns peer-to-peer. Physical GPS location is never tracked or stored.
  • Optional Account Backup: If you optionally register with Name and Email to back up preferences, your data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS solely for app functionality and account management.
  • Optional In-App Bug Reports: Stores report description, screen context, and app version to diagnose and fix software issues.

2. Non-Monetary Recreational Points Disclosure

All chips, points, and Session Hisaab tallies in Taash are strictly virtual, non-monetary scorekeeping units (0 INR cash value). Taash never accepts deposits, links to bank/UPI accounts, or offers real-money prizes, in compliance with India's Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025.

3. Data Sharing, Security & Deletion

We never sell or share user data with third parties. All transmissions use HTTPS encryption. Request data deletion anytime at amanjoshi668@gmail.com.
